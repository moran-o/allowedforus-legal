PRIVACY POLICY Effective Date: March 26, 2026 Version: 1.0 AllowedForUs ("App", "we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information. 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT a) Account Information When you sign in using Google, Facebook, or Apple, we receive and store: - Your name (as provided by your authentication provider) - Your email address - Your profile photo URL - Your unique user identifier - Your authentication provider (Google, Facebook, or Apple) b) User-Generated Content When you use the App, we collect: - Product votes you submit (which products you voted on and how you voted) - Product names you contribute - Product photographs you upload - Barcode scan and search activity c) Consent Records We store records of your acceptance of our Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and Health Disclaimer, including timestamps and document versions. d) Technical Information We may collect standard technical information including device type, operating system, and app version for debugging and improvement purposes. 2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION We use your information to: - Authenticate your identity and maintain your account - Display your profile information within the App (name, profile photo in the navigation menu) - Record and display your votes (votes are displayed in aggregate; individual votes are associated with your account but other users cannot see how you specifically voted) - Store and display product photos and names you contribute - Send push notifications about products or community activity - Improve the App and user experience - Enforce our Terms of Use - Comply with legal obligations 3. FIREBASE AND THIRD-PARTY SERVICES The App uses Google Firebase services, including: - Firebase Authentication (Google, Facebook, and Apple sign-in) - Cloud Firestore (storing user profiles, product data, consent records) - Firebase Realtime Database (real-time vote counts) - Firebase Storage (product images) - Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications) Your data is processed and stored on Google's Firebase infrastructure. Google's privacy practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Authentication providers (Google, Facebook, Apple) have their own privacy policies governing the data they share with us during sign-in. 4. DATA SHARING We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information: - With Firebase/Google as our infrastructure provider (as described above) - When required by law, regulation, or legal process - To protect the rights, property, or safety of AllowedForUs, our users, or others User-generated content (product names, photos, votes) is shared with all App users as part of the community database. Votes are displayed in aggregate form. 5. DATA RETENTION We retain your account information and user-generated content for as long as your account is active. Consent records are retained indefinitely for legal compliance purposes. 6. DATA SECURITY We use Firebase's built-in security features, including encrypted data transmission and Firebase Security Rules, to protect your data.
