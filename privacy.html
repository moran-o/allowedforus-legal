PRIVACY POLICY Effective Date: September 30, 2026 Version: 1.1 AllowedForUs (the "App") is operated by Ohana Labs ("we", "us", "our"), the controller of the database in which your personal information is kept. Contact: ohana.ai.apps@gmail.com. This Policy is provided in accordance with the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981. It explains what information the App processes, why, who receives it, and what account deletion removes or leaves behind. 1. YOUR CHOICES AND WHEN PROCESSING BEGINS You are under no legal obligation to provide information to us. Providing information and using the App are voluntary and depend on your consent. An account, and the information needed to authenticate it, is required for the App's signed-in features; if you choose not to provide it, those features will not be available. Voting, contributing content, reporting content, and enabling notifications are optional. Some processing starts before you reach the App's acceptance screen: your sign-in provider and Firebase process the information needed to sign you in and create your profile, and technical services such as crash reporting may start when the App opens. 2. INFORMATION WE PROCESS - Account information: your account identifier, sign-in provider, and the name, email address, and profile photo URL supplied by Google, Facebook, or Apple, where available. We also store your account settings and whether the account is restricted. We do not receive your Google, Facebook, or Apple password. - Contributions: product barcodes, names, descriptions, photos, votes, and requests for other users to rate a product. These records may include your account identifier and the time of creation or update. A photo starts uploading when you select it, before you finish saving the product. Photos may contain personal information; avoid including anything that is not needed to identify the product. - Searches: search terms and barcodes are sent to our backend to find products. The App does not keep a personal search or scan history. - Reports and moderation: a report includes your name and account identifier, the product, the reason, your comment if any, the time, and its status, and later the identifier of the administrator who handled it and the decision. If an account is blocked, we keep a block record with the account identifier, block status, time, the administrator's identifier, and the reason if one was entered. - Acceptance records: your account identifier, the document you accepted, its version, and the time. - Notifications: if you enable notifications, we store a delivery token, your notification settings, and the number and time of recent notifications sent to you. - Diagnostics: Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports, error details, device and app information, and installation identifiers. Our infrastructure providers may also process technical connection data, including IP addresses. - Sign-in components: the App includes the sign-in components of Google, Facebook, and Apple. These components may collect technical and usage information, such as device identifiers and app-launch events, under each provider's own policy. - On your device: the App stores your session and profile details, accepted document versions, language, and notification preferences. Signing out or deleting the account clears them, except the language preference. The App does not ask for and does not store medical information about you. Do not include information about your health in reports, product names, or photos. 3. WHY WE USE INFORMATION We use this information to authenticate accounts; provide product search and community contributions; process votes and publish live totals; send optional product notifications according to your preferences; handle reports and support requests; investigate misuse and enforce account restrictions; maintain a reliable service; record acceptance of the applicable documents; and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information. Reports and moderation information are used to operate and protect the service, not as public accusations against a user. 4. WHO CAN ACCESS INFORMATION Product information, photos, and vote totals are shared with all users. Product records and rating requests also carry the contributor's account identifier, which the apps of signed-in users can access even though the screens do not display it. Your individual votes, your full profile, and any block record are accessible only to you and to authorized administrators. Reports are accessible to the person who submitted them and to administrators. Administrators and those authorized to operate the backend can access the information needed to manage the service and investigate problems. Our infrastructure and sign-in providers process information in order to provide their services. We may disclose information when the law requires it, or when reasonably necessary to protect legal rights, users, or the service. 5. SERVICE PROVIDERS AND TRANSFER OUTSIDE ISRAEL The App uses Google Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, and Crashlytics for sign-in, data and image storage, vote processing, notifications, and diagnostics. Your sign-in provider also processes information under its own policy. The information is stored and processed on Google's servers, which may be located outside Israel, including in the United States. By accepting this Policy you consent to the transfer of your information outside Israel and to its processing there for the purpose of operating the service. See Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Firebase's privacy information (https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/). 6. RETENTION AND ACCOUNT DELETION While your account is active, we keep your account information and the records you submit in order to provide the service. You can delete your account from the App menu, from the blocked-account screen, or by contacting us. Deleting your account in the App removes your votes and their contribution to the vote totals, your profile, including the notification token stored in it, and your sign-in account with us. If the sign-in provider requires a recent sign-in, the App asks you to sign in again and repeat the deletion to complete it. Deleting your account does not automatically remove: - Product names, descriptions, and photos you contributed, which remain in the community database together with the contributor's account identifier. Photos you uploaded but did not save with a product are also not removed automatically. Contact us to ask for removal of content or of personal information in it. - Acceptance records, which document which versions were accepted and when. - Reports and their handling, rating requests, and technical records confirming the deletion. These may include your account identifier, your name as the reporter, and the report text. - A block record, if the account was blocked. It is kept until an administrator removes the block, so that the block continues to apply to the same account identifier if the profile is deleted or recreated. - Logs, backups, crash reports, and identifiers held by our providers, which follow the providers' own retention periods. Deleting your App account does not delete your Google, Facebook, or Apple account. These retained records currently have no automatic expiry date. You may ask us to delete them, and we will do so unless we have a lawful reason to keep them. 7. YOUR RIGHTS AND CONTROLS Under sections 13 and 14 of the Privacy Protection Law, you are entitled to inspect the information we hold about you and to ask us to correct or delete information that is not accurate, complete, clear, or up to date. You can also ask us to delete your account and the retained records described above. To make a request, contact ohana.ai.apps@gmail.com, including if you can no longer sign in. We may need to verify your identity before responding. You may withdraw your consent by contacting us or by deleting your account. Closing or uninstalling the App does not by itself delete your account. You can change notification frequency in the App and turn notifications off in your device settings. Camera and photo access can be controlled in your device settings; you can type a barcode instead of scanning it. You may contact the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority with any concern about how your information is handled. 8. SECURITY We use access controls and Firebase security features to protect information. No system can guarantee complete security. Protect your sign-in credentials and avoid uploading unnecessary personal or sensitive information. 9. CHILDREN AND MINORS The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly allow them to create accounts. Minors aged 13 to 17 may use the App only with the approval of a parent or legal guardian. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will take steps to remove it. 10.
